Dish 2.4.0: the N64 controller for Switch Online, and 32-bit Fire tablets.

• Nintendo 64 controller over USB-C and Bluetooth, with rumble, battery and player lights in Direct mode.
• Installs on 32-bit Android devices such as the Fire HD 8 (2018, 2020), Fire 7 (2019) and Fire HD 10 (2019).
• Android 11 and older: adding a Bluetooth PC lists paired devices and opens Bluetooth settings.
• Moonlight hosts: DualSense HD haptics play again; rumble and motion requests are no longer lost.
