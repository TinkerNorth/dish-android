Dish 2.2.0: DualSense haptics, light bar over Bluetooth, touchpad without Direct.

• A USB DualSense plays a game's haptic audio through its own actuators. Update Satellite too.
• The light bar follows the game over Bluetooth on Android 12 and newer.
• The DualShock 4 and DualSense touchpad works over Bluetooth and in USB Standard mode while Dish is on screen.
• A Direct controller shows its own battery.
• Controller sound and microphone no longer need Direct mode.
