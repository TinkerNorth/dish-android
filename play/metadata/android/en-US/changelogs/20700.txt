Dish 2.7.0: a controller list that updates itself, and a way to report a problem.

• Dish checks dish.tinkernorth.com for newer controller support. Settings has Check for controller updates automatically, on by default.
• Diagnostics shows each controller's record, known issues and last write; Report a problem sends your words and a file to support from your mail app.
• The controller inspector asks Did it work? after each test.
• Satellite needs no update.
