Dish 2.7.0: a controller list that updates itself, and a way to report a problem.

• Dish checks dish.tinkernorth.com for newer controller support (Settings, on by default).
• Diagnostics shows each controller's known issues and last write; Report a problem sends your words and a file to support.
• The inspector asks Did it work? after each test.
• Fixed: light bar, player LEDs, adaptive triggers, mic lamp, controller sound, haptics, mute button, Moonlight and Bluetooth Steam Controller.
