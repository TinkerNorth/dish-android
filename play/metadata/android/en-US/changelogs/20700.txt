Dish 2.7.0: a controller list that updates itself, and diagnostics you can email.

• Dish checks dish.tinkernorth.com for newer controller support. Settings has Check for controller updates automatically, on by default.
• Diagnostics shows each controller's record, known issues and its last write, and Email diagnostics sends a file to support from your mail app.
